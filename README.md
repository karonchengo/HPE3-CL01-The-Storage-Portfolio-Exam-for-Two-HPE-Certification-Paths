## What Is the HPE3-CL01 Storage Portfolio Exam?

The **HPE3-CL01 Storage Portfolio Exam** is a web-based continuous learning badge examination that validates candidates' understanding of the HPE Storage Portfolio coursework completed through HPE Tech Pro. Rather than focusing on a single storage product, it evaluates broader knowledge of HPE's storage portfolio and the learning materials associated with its products and solutions.

The exam is particularly relevant to professionals pursuing two different technical certification paths:
- **HPE ATP – Storage Solutions:** Develops specialized knowledge of HPE storage technologies and solutions.
- **HPE ATP – Edge-to-cloud architect:** Builds broader infrastructure knowledge across storage, compute, networking, and edge-to-cloud fundamentals.

For storage specialists, HPE3-CL01 provides foundational portfolio knowledge. For aspiring Edge-to-Cloud architects, it represents the storage component of a broader infrastructure certification journey.

## How HPE3-CL01 Fits into the Two HPE Certification Paths

One important feature of HPE3-CL01 is that it contributes to two different certification pathways. Although both require an understanding of HPE storage technologies, their overall learning objectives are different.

### HPE ATP – Storage Solutions

The **HPE ATP – Storage Solutions** certification is designed for professionals who want to develop technical knowledge of HPE storage products and solutions.

HPE3-CL01 is the first of three continuous learning badge examinations required for this certification.

The complete certification requirements include:

| Requirement | Exam or Course |
|---|---|
| 1 | Storage Portfolio Exam – HPE3-CL01 |
| 2 | Structured Data Solutions Exam – HPE3-CL02 |
| 3 | Hyperconverged Solutions Exam – HPE3-CL03 |
| 4 | HPE Storage Fundamentals – HPE0-J81 |

Candidates must complete all four requirements to earn the HPE ATP – Storage Solutions certification.

### HPE ATP – Edge-to-cloud architect

The **HPE ATP – Edge-to-cloud architect** certification takes a broader approach to infrastructure knowledge. It is designed for professionals who need to understand how different HPE technologies work together to support complete edge-to-cloud environments.

Storage Portfolio Exam HPE3-CL01 is one of three required badge examinations in this certification path.

The complete requirements include:

| Requirement | Exam or Course |
|---|---|
| 1 | Storage Portfolio Exam – HPE3-CL01 |
| 2 | Edge-to-Cloud - Compute Exam – HPE3-CL18 |
| 3 | HPE Edge-to-Cloud Fundamentals – HPE0-V18 |

Completing these requirements helps candidates build knowledge across storage, compute, and broader HPE infrastructure solutions.
